## 2. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Application collects, uses, stores and
protects information when you use it. It also explains your rights and the
choices available to you.

The Application is an **AI-assisted plant health and identification tool**. It
helps you identify plants, screen their visible condition from photos, and
offers educational and general care guidance. It is **not** a laboratory or
clinical diagnosis tool. Please read this policy together with our
[Terms & Conditions](TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md) and
[AI Disclaimer](AI_DISCLAIMER.md).

By installing or using the Application, you agree to the practices described in
this policy.

## 3. What PlantDoctor AI is and how it processes images

The Application analyses plant photos that **you** choose to capture with the
camera or select from your device's gallery.

- Photos are used only for the feature you requested (identification, visual
  health screening, saving plants, or viewing history).
- **As currently implemented, plant images are processed on your device.**
  PlantDoctor AI does not upload your photos to any external service, and
  current analysis runs locally on the phone.
- If, in a future version, any image is processed through the Application's
  own server infrastructure, this policy will be updated **before** that
  happens, and processing will be covered by the "On-device vs. online
  processing" section below as revised.

## 4. Information we collect

We collect only the minimum information needed for the features you use
(data minimisation):

| Category | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Plant photos** | Images you capture or select, and the working copies created to display and analyse them. |
| **Saved plant information** | Names, species, notes, and health history you choose to save in "My Plants". |
| **Scan history** | Past check-ups, their results, dates and thumbnails. This is local history you create. |
| **Device information** | Limited technical information needed for the app to run (for example, screen size and OS version used to render the interface). |
| **Usage / analytics information** | *None collected in the current version.* The app does not bundle analytics or tracking SDKs. If analytics are ever added, they will be described here and enabled only with your consent where required. |
| **Information you provide voluntarily** | Answers you type in the optional follow-up questions (for example, watering habits) and any notes you add to saved plants. |

We do **not** ask for, and do not require, your name, phone number, email
address or account to use the Application. **There is no registration and no
account requirement.**

## 5. How we use your information

Information is used solely to:

1. Run the analysis you request (plant identification and visual health
   screening);
2. Display results, evidence, care guidance and plant information;
3. Save, retrieve and delete plants and scan history as you direct;
4. Improve and maintain the Application (for example, fixing crashes or
   resolving unreadable images);
5. Respond to support or legal requests sent to the contact address above.

We do **not** use your photos for advertising, profiling, or selling data.

## 6. On-device vs. online processing

**Current version — on-device:**

- Analysis, identification and screening all run locally on your phone.
- No plant image is transmitted to the internet.
- Saving a scan stores data only in the local app database and app storage.

**Future versions:**

- The Application's architecture separates local functionality from any
  possible online AI functionality.
- If an online AI service is enabled in a future release, the app will
  require an internet connection for that specific feature, will tell you when
  an image is being sent for processing, and this section will be updated
  accordingly. API keys and server configuration will never be embedded in
  the app in a way that exposes secrets.

## 7. Third-party sharing

- **We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.**
- We do not share your photos or data with third parties for marketing.
- We may disclose information only where required or permitted by law (for

## 8. Your control and data deletion

You can control the data you store:

- **Delete saved plants** — from "My Plants".
- **Delete scan history** — from "History".
- **Delete the app** — uninstalling the Application removes the local database
  and the photos it stored in the app's storage area.

For any data deletion request, correction, or export request that you cannot
perform yourself, contact us at **vanshdhimang9@gmail.com** and we will help,
in line with applicable law.

- Data is stored in the Application's private storage on your device.
