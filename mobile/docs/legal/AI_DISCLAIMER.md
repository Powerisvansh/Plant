# PlantDoctor AI — AI Disclaimer

**Last updated:** 24 September 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This disclaimer explains the real capabilities and limits of PlantDoctor AI's
analysis. Please read it before relying on any result. It forms part of the
[Terms & Conditions](TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md).

## 1. What PlantDoctor AI is (and is not)

PlantDoctor AI is an **AI-assisted visual screening and educational tool**.
It analyses the *visible characteristics* of plant photos — leaf colour,
spots, brown areas, shape, texture and coverage — using an on-device image
analysis engine.

It is **not** a laboratory diagnosis, a scientific plant-health assessment
tool, or a replacement for a qualified professional, a plant clinic, an
agricultural extension service, or a laboratory test.

## 2. Results can be incorrect or uncertain

The Application does **not** guarantee:

- that a plant has been identified correctly;
- that the "PlantDoctor Health Index" reflects the true biological condition
  of the plant;
- that reported symptoms or suggested causes are correct;
- that "no problem detected" means the plant is genuinely healthy.

The Health Index is an **experimental software metric** based on visible
image characteristics. It is not a universally accepted standard, and a
change in the index does **not** by itself prove that a plant's health has
improved or deteriorated biologically.

## 3. Photo limitations

The analysis is limited by what a photo can show:

- **Poor image quality** — dark, blurry, obstructed or out-of-focus photos
  reduce accuracy. The Application will warn you when it detects such
  conditions, and may still produce a result with reduced reliability.
- **Missing context** — the camera cannot see roots, soil, watering history,
  weather, or the plant's environment. These factors frequently separate one
  cause of a symptom from another.
- **Similar-looking symptoms** — many different conditions (fungal, bacterial,
  nutrient, pest, water and environmental) can appear visually similar in a
  photograph.

Because of these limits, the Application reports **possible causes** and
**hypotheses**, and never claims a confirmed diagnosis.

## 4. Identification is a hint, not a certainty

When the Application suggests a plant identity, it may be based on leaf-shape
morphology heuristics and general characteristics. If it cannot determine a
plant reliably, it says so explicitly ("Plant identification is uncertain").
A suggested species name is a **candidate for you to verify against your own
plant**, not a definitive identification.

## 5. What to do with a result

- Treat every result as a starting point for your own checking.
- Inspect the plant, its soil, leaves and environment directly.
- For anything serious — including decisions about chemicals, pesticides,
  fungicides, large crops, or valuable plants — **confirm with an appropriate
  expert** (a local agricultural extension service, plant clinic, or qualified
  professional) before taking action that could harm the plant, your health,
  or the environment.
- Always follow product labels and local regulatory guidance for any
  plant-protection product, and take appropriate safety precautions.

## 6. Treatment guidance is conservative by design

The Application prioritises general care and low-risk practical actions. It
does not prescribe chemicals as guaranteed treatments, and never provides
hazardous mixing instructions. No treatment suggestion in the Application
should be treated as a prescription.

## 7. Science-fair results are not real-world validation

The built-in science-fair demonstration measures the analysis engine on a
labelled synthetic test dataset processed on the device. It demonstrates the
engine's internal consistency and honesty in a controlled demonstration — it
does **not** represent validated real-world accuracy on real photographs.
